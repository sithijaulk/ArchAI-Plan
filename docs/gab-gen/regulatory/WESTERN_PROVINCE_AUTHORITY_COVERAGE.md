**Total Local Authorities Registered:** 47 Authorities (5 Municipal Councils, 14 Urban Councils, 28 Pradeshiya Sabhas)  
**Planning Status:** 100% of Western Province is within UDA Declared Urban Planning Jurisdictions.

## 4. Key Rules of Jurisdiction Resolution

1. **UDA General Applicability:**
   - Under Gazette Extraordinary No. 2235/54, standard building, room dimension, ventilation, staircase, and setback regulations apply automatically across **ALL 47 local authorities** in the Western Province unless a more restrictive approved development plan applies.
2. **Kaduwela & Other Specific Plans:**
   - While Kaduwela has an approved Development Plan, it adopts UDA Gazette 2235/54 standard schedules for residential low-rise developments. UDA regulations are **never** restricted to Kaduwela alone.
3. **Colombo Municipal Council (CMC):**
   - The City of Colombo Development Plan applies **only** to the Colombo Municipal Council geographic boundary. CMC rules (Form C1 and C2) are **not** generalized to outer councils (e.g. Kaduwela, Dehiwala, Gampaha) without statutory decree.
