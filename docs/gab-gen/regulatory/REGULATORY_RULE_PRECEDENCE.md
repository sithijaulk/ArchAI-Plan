## 1. Statutory Precedence Hierarchy

## 2. Deterministic Conflict Resolution Rules

1. **National vs. Local Conflict:**
   - Where a site falls within an approved Development Plan (e.g., CMC limits), local provisions explicitly enacted under Section 8F of Law No. 41 of 1978 prevail over general national provisions on the same subject matter (e.g., Form C2 setbacks in CMC prevail within CMC).
   - In the absence of an area-specific Development Plan provision, Gazette No. 2235/54 applies automatically as the mandatory baseline.
2. **Later Amendments:**
   - An extraordinary gazette amendment (e.g., Gazette 2105/24 amending the Colombo Plan) modifies only the specific clauses cited. All unamended clauses of the principal enactment remain legally binding.
3. **Language Inconsistencies:**
   - In accordance with Article 23(1) of the Constitution of Sri Lanka, the Sinhala text is the authoritative legal record. If an unofficial English draft or translation diverges from the Sinhala Gazette No. 2235/54, the Sinhala Gazette rule is enforced.
4. **Advisory / Draft Non-Activation:**
   - Documents categorized as drafts, proposed amendments (e.g. `gSllLrdzez.pdf` parking proposals), or advisory division guidelines (e.g. `Y495kC5iPp.pdf` PORS) must **NOT** be activated as mandatory statutory blocking rules. They may only produce informational advisory notes.
5. **Special Environmental Overlays:**
   - Environmental constraints from SLLDC (canal reservations), Agrarian Development Department (paddy prohibition), or Coastal Conservation Department operate concurrently with building bylaws. The most restrictive building setback or prohibition takes precedence on affected boundaries.

## 3. Mandatory Professional Review Flagging

Automated checks produce a verified regulatory compliance assessment, but **cannot grant legal planning permission**. Any plan generated or edited must be marked:
- `verified_compliant`: Every automated mathematical and regulatory rule passed against verified gazetted provisions.
- `requires_professional_review`: Requires formal sign-off by a Chartered Architect / Qualified Structural Engineer / Licensed Surveyor prior to authority submission.
- `requires_regulatory_verification`: Specific local authority street line or site-specific reservation remains unverified from survey deed.
