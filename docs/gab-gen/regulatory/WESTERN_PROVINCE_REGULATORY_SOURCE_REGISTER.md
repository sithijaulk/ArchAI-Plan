## 2. Additional Statutory & Regulatory Frameworks Researched

### 2.1 Urban Development Authority Law No. 41 of 1978 & Gazette 2235/54
- **Primary Instrument:** Gazette Extraordinary No. 2235/54 dated 8 July 2021.
- **Geographic Applicability:** Applies to all declared urban areas in Sri Lanka. Under UDA Declaration (2024), **all Local Authorities in the Western Province are declared areas**.
- **Key Deterministic Mandates:**
  - **Schedule 6 Form C:**
    - Low-Rise Residential (Height < 7m, <= 2 floors): Max plot coverage **65%**, Min rear space **2.3m**, Min side space **1.0m** (if window openings face boundary) or **0.0m** (blind wall with no openings, up to 2 storeys / 7m).
    - Front setback: **3.0m** from center line or road boundary depending on street line declaration.
  - **Schedule 7 [Regulation 66] — Minimum Habitable Room Dimensions:**
    - Single room in dwelling unit: Min area **11.0 m²**, Min width **3.0 m**.
    - First bedroom (more than 1 room): Min area **9.5 m²**, Min width **2.5 m**.
    - Additional bedrooms: Min area **8.5 m²**, Min width **2.5 m**.
    - Kitchen: Min area **5.5 m²**, Min width **1.8 m**.
    - Bathroom: Min area **1.7 m²**, Min width **1.0 m**.
    - Toilet (Water Closet): Min area **1.7 m²**, Min width **1.0 m**.
    - Combined Bath & Toilet: Min area **2.0 m²**, Min width **1.5 m**.
    - Living Room: Must satisfy minimum habitable room area (>= 9.5 m², min width 2.5 m).
  - **Regulation 67 — Room Ceilings & Clear Heights:**
    - Living rooms and bedrooms: Average **2.8 m**, minimum at lowest point **2.4 m**.
    - Kitchen: Average **2.8 m**, minimum at lowest point **2.4 m**.
    - Bathrooms, toilets, corridors, verandas: Minimum **2.1 m**.
    - Covered footways and stairways: Minimum clear headroom **2.1 m**.
  - **Schedule 8 [Regulation 68] — Staircases:**
    - Internal stairs serving only one upper floor: Min width **750 mm**, Max riser **175 mm**, Min tread **250 mm**, Min headroom **2.1 m**.
    - Stairs in public/condominium buildings: Min width **1000 mm**, Max riser **150 mm**, Min tread **275 mm**, Min headroom **2.1 m**.
  - **Regulation 70 — Natural Lighting and Ventilation:**
    - Window/opening area into habitable rooms: Minimum **1/5 (20%)** of floor space unobstructed.
    - Bathrooms, toilets, utility rooms: Minimum **1/10 (10%)** or light well complying with Schedule 10.
  - **Schedule 10 [Regulation 71] — Light Wells / Air Wells:**
    - Habitable rooms (Height < 15m): Min clear width **3.0 m**, Min area **9.0 m²**.
    - Utility rooms / toilets: Min clear width **1.0 m**, Min area **1.0 m²**.
  - **Schedule 11 & 12 — Parking Requirements:**
    - Single dwelling house: **1 car parking space** required (+1 additional if total floor area exceeds 400 m²).
    - Standard car stall dimensions: Min **2.5 m × 5.0 m** (perpendicular / angle).
  - **Roof Regulations (Regulations 46(3), 57(3), 67(6)(b)):**
    - Roof terrace area must be accounted in total permissible development.
    - Parapet / Guard Wall: Min **2.0 m** guard wall on adjoining property boundary if roof terrace/balcony is used up to boundary. Standard safety parapet **1.0 m – 1.1 m**.
    - Roof projection / overhang: Max **0.6m to 1.0m** projection into side setback; must not overhang across the legal boundary line onto adjoining land.

### 2.2 City of Colombo Development Plan (q31LejWXNw.pdf & svjKKqKG6J.pdf)
- Applies **strictly within Colombo Municipal Council (CMC)** limits.
- **Form C1:** Controls FAR (Floor Area Ratio) based on road width and land extent.
- **Form C2 (Revised by Gazette 2054/45 & 2105/24):**
  - Low Rise (Ground floor, 2 storeys): Min site frontage 6.0m, Max plot coverage 65%, Rear space 2.3m, Side space: GF only with blind wall 0m, or 1.0m/2.3m depending on window openings.
  - 3–4 storeys: Rear space 3.0m, side space according to table.

### 2.3 Local Authority Bylaws (Municipal Councils, Urban Councils, Pradeshiya Sabhas)
- **Municipal Councils Ordinance (Cap 252)**
- **Urban Councils Ordinance (Cap 255)**
- **Pradeshiya Sabhas Act No. 15 of 1987**
- Where a site is in an Urban Council or Pradeshiya Sabha within Western Province, the **UDA General Regulations (Gazette 2235/54)** serve as the governing development control standard pursuant to Section 8 of Law No. 41 of 1978, enforced through delegated authority by the local council.

### 2.4 Environmental, Wetland & Drainage Regulations
- **Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation (SLLDC) Act No. 52 of 1968 & Amendments:**
  - Canal and drainage reservations: 4.5m to 10m from high water edge depending on canal category.
  - Low-lying flood detention: Filling without SLLDC clearance is strictly prohibited.
- **Agrarian Development Act No. 46 of 2000:**
  - Mandatory restriction on constructing upon gazetted paddy lands without Agrarian Development Department clearance.
- **Western Province Wetlands Zoning Guidelines (2006):**
  - Wetland Nature Conservation / Protection Zones: Residential buildings not permitted or strictly on stilts (7m max height, visually compatible roofscape, no solid boundary walls).

## 3. Discrepancy & Precedence Protocol

1. **Statutory Hierarchy:**
   - Level 1: Act of Parliament (UDA Law No. 41 of 1978, SLLDC Act, NEA).
   - Level 2: Extraordinary Gazettes published by the Government of Sri Lanka (Gazette No. 2235/54 of 2021, Gazette No. 2105/24 of 2019).
   - Level 3: Approved Gazette Development Plans (City of Colombo Dev Plan).
   - Level 4: Statutory Local Authority By-laws.
   - Level 5: Advisory guidelines and draft proposals (e.g. 2006 Wetlands Guidelines, PORS Guidelines, Parking floor proposals).

2. **Language Discrepancy:**
   - In accordance with Article 23(1) of the Constitution of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, in the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and English texts of any statutory Gazette, the **Sinhala text shall prevail**.
   - All critical threshold values in this knowledge base are verified directly against Sinhala Gazette 2235/54.
